## Selected Engineering Cases

### MAGMA — Enterprise AI Orchestration

B2B intelligence platform designed for concurrent autonomous workflows.

- Portier/Worker orchestration with strict idempotence and state guarantees
- Multi-tenant PostgreSQL with RLS, immutable auditability, and Zero-PII lifecycle
- LLM/RAG pipelines using `pgvector`, structural validation, and anti-hallucination safeguards

`FastAPI` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `n8n` `LLM/RAG`

### CHAMIONG — Hybrid Headless B2B Platform

Digital decoupling of an industrial company's presentation layer from its operational Odoo ERP.

- Next.js and Sanity headless architecture with resilient Odoo JSON-RPC connectors
- SSR with authenticated price and stock gating
- FR / EN / AR localization, RTL support, programmatic SEO, and approximately 95% client autonomy

`Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Sanity` `Odoo` `JSON-RPC`

### PREVIZMA — B2B Sales Forecasting

Distributed decision-support system that transforms ERP-style sales data into interpretable commercial forecasts.

- Spring Boot business and security core with an isolated FastAPI ML service
- B2B segmentation, What-If simulations, operational alerts, and manager-oriented KPIs
- Backtesting, model comparison, explainability, and fallback forecasting

`Spring Boot` `FastAPI` `Angular` `PostgreSQL` `LightGBM` `XGBoost`

### DXC — Competency Decision System

Management system for production-team competency coverage within the Insurance Service Line.

- Data-pipeline validation and business-rule modelling
- Power BI decision indicators, management dashboards, and maintainable data-entry model

`Power BI` `DAX` `Power Apps` `Dataverse` `Data Modelling`
